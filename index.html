About the Book

Henry is a criminal who was found guilty of murder in the highest degree. In the year 3401 A.D. he was given an option in court to go back in time and collect 4 feathers needed for the royal court of Noxsis. They are spread across desert, mountains, forests, and plains with each feather falling within the borders of 4 kingdoms.

Genre: Fiction
